System Pipeline

How NetSentinel AI Protects Your Perimeter

From raw wire packet frames to classified security telemetry in three deterministic steps.

Step 01

Wire Capture & Dissection

Ingests real-time network packets from interface taps or offline CSV dumps. Dissects headers into IP addresses, ports, protocol types, flag configurations, and payload lengths.

Step 02

Feature Vector Extraction

Maps network telemetry across 41 connection attributes, computing flow duration, error rates, service categories, and host connection histories matching NSL-KDD standards.

Step 03

AI Prediction & Mitigation

Evaluates features against trained Random Forest trees in sub-2ms time. High-confidence intrusions trigger automated severity alerts and persist forensic records to the audit log.
